Adjustment rule: Don't change calories based on one weigh-in. After at least 14 days: if your 7-day average is falling around 0.6–1.0 kg/week, keep the plan. If you are consistently losing more than ~1 kg/week and recovery/strength is poor, add 100–150 kcal. If you are losing under ~0.5 kg/week for two straight weeks despite accurate tracking, reduce 100–150 kcal or add roughly 2,000 steps/day—not both at once.
